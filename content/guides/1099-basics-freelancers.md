If you're an independent contractor, clients don't withhold taxes from your pay. Instead, some clients report what they paid you to the IRS on a **Form 1099** (commonly Form 1099-NEC for nonemployee compensation).

## What to know
- **You owe tax on all your self-employment income, whether or not you get a 1099.** A missing form doesn't mean the income isn't taxable.
- Clients often ask you for a **Form W-9** with your name and taxpayer ID (SSN or EIN) before paying you.
- Payment apps and card processors may send a **Form 1099-K** instead.
- Reporting thresholds have changed in recent years. Check the IRS's [independent contractor forms page](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/forms-and-associated-taxes-for-independent-contractors) for current rules instead of trusting old blog posts.
