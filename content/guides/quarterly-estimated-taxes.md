---
title: "Quarterly estimated taxes for new freelancers"
date: 2026-10-14
---
When no employer withholds taxes for you, the IRS generally expects you to pay **estimated taxes** during the year, which covers income tax and self-employment tax. See the IRS's [estimated taxes page](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/estimated-taxes).

## Plain-English steps
1. Track income and expenses from day one.
2. Set aside a percentage of every payment in a separate savings account. Many freelancers pick a cautious share; an accountant can help you set yours.
3. Use IRS Form 1040-ES to calculate and pay. Due dates are listed on the [IRS page](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/estimated-taxes).
4. Check your state: many states have their own estimated tax.

Not tax advice. A one-time session with a tax professional in your first year is money well spent.
