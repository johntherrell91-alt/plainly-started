Both handle invoicing. They differ in focus.

- **FreshBooks** (<a href="#AFFILIATE-freshbooks" rel="sponsored nofollow">try it</a> <!-- PLACEHOLDER affiliate link: replace after approval -->): accounting-first. Invoicing, expenses, time tracking, reports. Good if you want bookkeeping in one place.
- **Bonsai** (<a href="#AFFILIATE-bonsai" rel="sponsored nofollow">try it</a> <!-- PLACEHOLDER affiliate link: replace after approval -->): a freelancer workflow suite. Proposals, contracts with e-signature, invoicing, time tracking.

## Choose FreshBooks if…
you mainly need clean books and invoices, or you work with an accountant.

## Choose Bonsai if…
you send many proposals and contracts and want them linked to invoices.

Both offer trials. Pricing changes, so check their sites. If you only send a few invoices a month, a free spreadsheet template may be enough.
