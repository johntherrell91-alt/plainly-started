## Is it legally required?
For a sole proprietor, generally no. For an LLC, keeping business money separate is strongly recommended. Mixing funds can weaken the liability protection you formed the LLC for.

## Why it helps everyone
- Clean records at tax time
- Easier to spot deductible expenses
- Looks more professional to clients

## What banks usually ask for
Requirements vary by bank. Commonly:
- A government ID
- Your EIN or SSN (get a free EIN from the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number))
- For an LLC, your formation documents and sometimes an operating agreement
