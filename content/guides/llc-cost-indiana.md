---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Indiana?"
date: 2026-10-21
---
Forming an Indiana LLC costs **$95 online** through INBiz (or **$100 on paper**), and after that you file a **business entity report every two years** for **$32 online** ($50 on paper). That makes Indiana one of the cheaper states to run an LLC in.

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $95 online, $100 on paper.** Indiana Code sets the electronic filing fee at $75 and the paper fee at $100 ([IC 23-0.5-9-19](https://iga.in.gov/ic/2026/Title_23/Article_0.5/Chapter_9.pdf)). Online filings also carry a $20 enhanced access fee ([75 IAC 8-3-4](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/indiana/75-IAC-8-3-4)), which is why INBiz shows $95. The paper form is [State Form 49459](https://forms.in.gov/Download.aspx?id=16989).
- **Card processing fee:** INBiz adds a small checkout fee of at least $1 and no more than 2.15%. Source: [INBiz fee calculator](https://inbiz.in.gov/Inbiz/FeeCalculator/Index).
- **Business entity report: $32 online, $50 on paper,** every two years. Source: [INBiz business entity reports](https://inbiz.in.gov/business-filings/business-entityreport).

## When the business entity report is due
For-profit entities, including LLCs, file every other year in the **anniversary month** the LLC was formed, and you can file up to 90 days before that month. Source: [Indiana Secretary of State: business entity reports](https://www.in.gov/sos/business/business-entity-reports/).

## Registered agent
The Articles of Organization must name a registered agent, either a commercial registered agent or a noncommercial agent with an Indiana street address, and you confirm the agent has consented. Source: [State Form 49459](https://forms.in.gov/Download.aspx?id=16989).

## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$96 to $97** online once the card fee is added, or $100 by mail. Then about **$32 every two years** for the business entity report. Confirm current fees with the [INBiz fee calculator](https://inbiz.in.gov/Inbiz/FeeCalculator/Index) before filing, since they can change.
