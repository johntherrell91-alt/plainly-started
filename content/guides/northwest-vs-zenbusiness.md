Both companies will file your LLC paperwork. Neither is required: you can always file directly with your state for just the state fee.

| | Northwest Registered Agent | ZenBusiness |
|---|---|---|
| Known for | Privacy (their address on filings), phone support | Low-cost starter plan, add-on compliance tools |
| Registered agent | Included for the first year with formation (per the company) | Included in higher tiers (per the company) |
| Best for | People who want home-address privacy | People who want bundled reminders and add-ons |

*Prices and inclusions change often, so check each provider's current pricing page.*

## How to choose
1. If home-address privacy matters most, look at <a href="#AFFILIATE-northwest" rel="sponsored nofollow">Northwest</a> <!-- PLACEHOLDER affiliate link: replace after approval -->.
2. If you want the lowest upfront service price and plan to add tools later, look at <a href="#AFFILIATE-zenbusiness" rel="sponsored nofollow">ZenBusiness</a> <!-- PLACEHOLDER affiliate link: replace after approval -->.
3. If you're comfortable with forms, file yourself and save the service fee.

Either way, the **state filing fee is extra** and goes to the state.
