---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Minnesota?"
date: 2026-10-22
---
Forming a Minnesota LLC costs **$155 online or in person** or **$135 by mail**, and the **annual renewal is free**. You just have to file it by **December 31** each year, starting the year after you form.

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $155 online or in person, $135 by mail.** Source: [Minnesota Secretary of State fee schedule](https://www.sos.mn.gov/business-liens/start-a-business/business-filing-certification-fee-schedule/).
- **Annual renewal: $0.** Minnesota law says no fee is required. Source: [Minn. Stat. § 322C.0208](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/322C.0208).
- **Reinstatement if you miss the renewal: $25 by mail, $45 online or in person.** Source: [fee schedule](https://www.sos.mn.gov/business-liens/start-a-business/business-filing-certification-fee-schedule/).

## When the annual renewal is due
Every calendar year beginning the year after you file your Articles, the renewal is due by **December 31**. Skipping it leads to administrative termination of the LLC. Sources: [Minn. Stat. § 322C.0208](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/322C.0208) and [how to renew your business filing](https://www.sos.mn.gov/business-liens/start-a-business/how-to-renew-or-amend-your-business-filing).

## First-year total if you do it yourself
**$135 by mail or $155 online**, and then **$0 a year** as long as you file the renewal on time. Confirm current fees with the [Minnesota Secretary of State](https://www.sos.mn.gov/business-liens/start-a-business/business-filing-certification-fee-schedule/) before filing, since they can change.
