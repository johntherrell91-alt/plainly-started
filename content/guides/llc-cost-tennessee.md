---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Tennessee?"
date: 2026-10-22
---
Forming a Tennessee LLC costs **at least $300**, because the state charges **$50 per member with a $300 minimum** (and a $3,000 cap). The annual report costs the same, so a small LLC pays **$300 every year**, and most LLCs also owe at least **$100 a year in franchise tax**. That makes Tennessee one of the more expensive states for a small LLC.

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $50 per member, minimum $300, maximum $3,000.** An LLC with one to six members pays $300. Source: [Tennessee Articles of Organization, Form SS-4270](https://sos-prod.tnsosgovfiles.com/s3fs-public/document/SS-4270%20LLC.pdf?VersionId=ud_ACjrIyFDBjfjOATyeux2Nqnechtxv) and [Tenn. Code § 48-249-1007](https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/limited-liability-companies/chapter-249/part-10/section-48-249-1007/).
- **Card or e-check convenience fee:** paying online or in person adds a convenience fee. You can avoid it by mailing the form with a check or money order payable to the Tennessee Secretary of State. Source: [Form SS-4270 instructions](https://sos-prod.tnsosgovfiles.com/s3fs-public/document/SS-4270%20LLC.pdf?VersionId=ud_ACjrIyFDBjfjOATyeux2Nqnechtxv).
- **Annual report: $300 minimum, plus $50 per member over six, up to $3,000.** Changing your registered agent or office on the report adds $20. Source: [Tennessee Secretary of State FAQs](https://sos.tn.gov/businesses/faqs).

## When the annual report is due
It's due on or before the **first day of the fourth month after your fiscal year ends**. If you don't pick a fiscal year on your Articles, the state uses December, so the report is due **April 1**. Source: [Tenn. Code § 48-249-1017](https://law.justia.com/codes/tennessee/title-48/limited-liability-companies/chapter-249/part-10/section-48-249-1017/) and [TNBear annual report instructions](https://tnbear.tn.gov/Ecommerce/AnnualReportInstr.aspx).

## Franchise and excise tax
Tennessee taxes most LLCs separately from the Secretary of State fees:
- **Franchise tax:** 0.25% of net worth, with a **$100 minimum** owed every year you're registered, even if the LLC is inactive.
- **Excise tax:** 6.5% of net earnings, with no minimum.

A single-member LLC that's disregarded for federal taxes is **still** subject to these taxes in Tennessee unless its owner is a corporation. Sources: [Tennessee Department of Revenue: franchise and excise tax](https://www.tn.gov/revenue/taxes/franchise---excise-tax.html) and [F&E-3: disregarded entities](https://revenue.support.tn.gov/hc/en-us/articles/360057842032-F-E-3-Filing-Requirements-for-Disregarded-Entities).

## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$300** to form (plus a convenience fee if you pay online), then roughly **$400 a year** after that: the $300 annual report plus at least $100 in franchise tax. Because sole proprietors don't owe franchise and excise tax, it's worth reading [LLC vs. sole proprietor](../llc-vs-sole-proprietor/) before you file. Confirm current fees with the [Tennessee Secretary of State](https://sos.tn.gov/businesses/faqs), since they can change.
