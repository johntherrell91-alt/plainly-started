date: 2026-10-14
---
## State fees
- **Articles of Organization: $200** ([NY Department of State](https://dos.ny.gov/forming-limited-liability-company-new-york))
- **Certificate of Publication: $50** ([NY DOS](https://dos.ny.gov/forming-limited-liability-company-new-york))
- Biennial Statement: $9 ([NY DOS fee schedule](https://dos.ny.gov/fee-schedules))

## The publication requirement
Most New York LLCs must publish a notice in **two newspapers designated by the county clerk, for six consecutive weeks**, then file the Certificate of Publication within **120 days**. If you don't, the LLC's authority to do business is suspended ([NY DOS](https://dos.ny.gov/forming-limited-liability-company-new-york)).

The newspapers set their own prices, and the state doesn't publish them, so costs vary a lot by county. Call the newspapers your county clerk designates for quotes before you form.

## Other costs
EIN: free from the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number).
