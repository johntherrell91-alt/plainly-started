---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Pennsylvania?"
date: 2026-10-16
---
Forming a Pennsylvania LLC costs **$125** in state fees for the Certificate of Organization, according to the [Pennsylvania Department of State fee schedule](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dos/programs/business/fees-and-payments).

## What you pay the state
- **Certificate of Organization: $125.** File with the Bureau of Corporations and Charitable Organizations (online through Business Filing Services or by mail with form DSCB:15-8821 and a docketing statement). Source: [PA DOS fees and payments](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dos/programs/business/fees-and-payments).
- **Annual report: $7 per year** for for-profit LLCs ($0 if the LLC has a not-for-profit purpose). Pennsylvania began requiring these annual reports in 2025. Source: same fee schedule and the [Annual Reports page](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dos/programs/business/types-of-filings-and-registrations/annual-reports).
- LLC annual reports are due in the **January 1 to September 30** window each year (per Department of State annual report instructions).

## Registered office
Pennsylvania filings need a registered office address in the state. ## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$125** to form, then **$7 a year** for the for-profit annual report. Fees can change, so confirm on the official fee schedule before filing.
