---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Illinois?"
date: 2026-10-17
---
Forming an Illinois LLC costs **$150** in state fees for Articles of Organization, as set in [805 ILCS 180/50-10](https://www.ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/080501800K50-10.htm) and listed by the [Illinois Secretary of State](https://www.ilsos.gov/departments/business-services/organization/llc-instructions.html).

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $150** for standard processing (about 10 business days), or **$250** for 24-hour service. The Secretary of State also adds a card payment processor fee when you file online. Source: [ILSOS online LLC instructions](https://www.ilsos.gov/departments/business-services/organization/llc-instructions.html).
- **Series LLC: $400** (or $500 expedited) if you want an LLC that can set up series. Most freelancers don't need this. Source: [ILSOS online LLC instructions](https://www.ilsos.gov/departments/business-services/organization/llc-instructions.html).
- **Annual report: $75.** Source: [805 ILCS 180/50-10](https://www.ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/080501800K50-10.htm) and the [ILSOS LLC forms list](https://www.ilsos.gov/publications/business_services/llc.html).

## When the annual report is due
Under [805 ILCS 180/50-1](https://www.ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/080501800K50-1.htm), the annual report is due **within the 60 days before the first day of your LLC's anniversary month** (the month it was formed). So if you form in October, you'd file each year in the window ending October 1.

Illinois LLCs must keep a registered agent with an Illinois address. ## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$150** to form (plus the small card processing fee online). Then **$75 a year** for the annual report. Confirm fees on the official ILSOS pages before filing, since they can change.
