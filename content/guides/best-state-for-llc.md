---
title: "Best state to form an LLC if you work from home"
date: 2026-10-14
---
You'll see ads for Delaware or Wyoming. For most home-based freelancers, **your own state is usually the simplest choice.**

## Why
If you live and work in State A but form in State B, State A typically still considers you "doing business" there. You may then have to **register as a foreign LLC** in your home state, paying two sets of fees and needing two registered agents.

Example: California says an LLC "doing business or organized in California" owes the [$800 annual tax](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/limited-liability-company/index.html), so forming elsewhere doesn't avoid it if you do business in California.

## When another state might make sense
Larger companies raising investment, or holding companies, sometimes have reasons. That's a conversation for an attorney.
