---
title: "Freelance contract checklist: 10 clauses you need"
date: 2026-10-14
---
1. **Parties**: legal names of you and the client.
2. **Scope of work**: exactly what's included, and what isn't.
3. **Deliverables and deadlines.**
4. **Payment terms**: rate, deposit, due dates, late fees.
5. **Revisions**: how many are included, and the price of extras.
6. **Intellectual property**: when ownership transfers (often on full payment).
7. **Confidentiality.**
8. **Termination**: how either side can exit, and the payment for work done.
9. **Independent contractor status**: you're not an employee.
10. **Dispute resolution and governing law.**

*Not legal advice. For high-value contracts, have an attorney review your template once and reuse it.*
