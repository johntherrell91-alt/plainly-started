---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Maryland?"
date: 2026-10-21
---
Forming a Maryland LLC costs **$100** for the Articles of Organization, then **$300 a year** for the Form 1 annual report, due **April 15**. Both fees are paid to the Maryland State Department of Assessments and Taxation (SDAT).

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $100.** Source: [SDAT Articles of Organization form](https://dat.maryland.gov/SDAT%20Forms/artorgan.pdf) and [SDAT list of fees](https://dat.maryland.gov/Documents/Accessible%20Documents/Charter%20-%20Resource%20Docs/List%20of%20Fees%20for%20Charter%20Services%20and%20Document%20Filings_0326-A.pdf).
- **Expedited service: $50,** optional. Source: [SDAT list of fees](https://dat.maryland.gov/Documents/Accessible%20Documents/Charter%20-%20Resource%20Docs/List%20of%20Fees%20for%20Charter%20Services%20and%20Document%20Filings_0326-A.pdf).
- **Form 1 annual report: $300.** Source: [2026 Form 1](https://dat.maryland.gov/Documents/Accessible%20Documents/BPP%20-%20Annual%20Reports%20and%20Returns/2026%20Filings%20-%20Form%201%20Business%20Entity%20Annual%20Report_0426-A.pdf).

## When the annual report is due
Every Maryland LLC files Form 1 (the annual report and business personal property return) by **April 15** each year. You can request a 60-day extension online. Source: [SDAT businesses page](https://dat.maryland.gov/businesses/Pages/default.aspx).

## A way to skip the $300 fee
Businesses approved by MarylandSaves, the state's retirement savings program, can get the $300 annual report fee waived for each year they stay qualified. Source: [SDAT businesses page](https://dat.maryland.gov/businesses/Pages/default.aspx).

## Resident (registered) agent
Maryland calls it a **resident agent**. It can be a Maryland citizen over 18, a Maryland corporation, or a Maryland LLC, its address can't be a P.O. box, and the agent signs the Articles of Organization. Source: [SDAT Articles of Organization instructions](https://dat.maryland.gov/SDAT%20Forms/artorgan.pdf).

## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$100** to form ($150 expedited), then **$300 every April 15** unless you qualify for the MarylandSaves waiver. Confirm current fees with [SDAT](https://dat.maryland.gov/businesses/Pages/default.aspx) before filing, since they can change.
