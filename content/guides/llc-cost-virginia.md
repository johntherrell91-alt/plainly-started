---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Virginia?"
date: 2026-10-19
---
Forming a Virginia LLC costs **$100** to file Articles of Organization with the State Corporation Commission (SCC), then **$50 a year** for the annual registration fee (Virginia does not require an annual report for LLCs). Sources: [Virginia SCC LLC forms and fees](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/businesses/forms-and-fees/virginia-limited-liability-companies/) and [SCC annual registration fees](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/businesses/business-faqs/annual-registration-fees/).

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $100.** File online through the SCC Clerk's Information System (CIS) or use Form LLC1011 by mail or in person. Source: [Virginia SCC LLC forms and fees](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/businesses/forms-and-fees/virginia-limited-liability-companies/) and [§ 13.1-1005](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title13.1/chapter12/section13.1-1005/).
- **Annual registration fee: $50.** Due by the last day of the month your LLC was organized, each year after formation. There is no separate annual report filing for Virginia LLCs—just the fee. Source: [SCC annual registration fees](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/businesses/business-faqs/annual-registration-fees/) and [§ 13.1-1062](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title13.1/chapter12/section13.1-1062/).
- **Late penalty: $25** if the registration fee is not paid by the due date. If it stays unpaid through the last day of the third month after the due date, the LLC is automatically canceled. Source: [SCC annual registration fees](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/businesses/business-faqs/annual-registration-fees/).
- **Name reservation: $10,** optional, for 120 days. Source: [Virginia SCC LLC forms and fees](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/businesses/forms-and-fees/virginia-limited-liability-companies/).

## When the annual registration fee is due
The SCC assesses the fee about two months before it is due and sends notice to the registered agent. You cannot pay before that assessment. The due date is the last day of your anniversary month (the month you organized). Source: [SCC annual registration fees](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/businesses/business-faqs/annual-registration-fees/).

## Registered agent
Every Virginia LLC must keep a registered agent and a Virginia registered office. An individual agent must be a Virginia resident who qualifies (for example, a member or manager of the LLC, or a member of the Virginia State Bar), or you can appoint an authorized commercial service company. The LLC cannot be its own registered agent. Source: [§ 13.1-1015](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title13.1/chapter12/section13.1-1015/) and [SCC registered agent FAQs](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/businesses/business-faqs/registered-agents/).

About **$100** to form. Then **$50 a year** for the annual registration fee (first assessment is roughly one year after you organize). Confirm current fees with the [Virginia SCC](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/businesses/forms-and-fees/virginia-limited-liability-companies/) before filing, since they can change.
