## Using your SSN
Sole proprietors with no employees can generally use their Social Security number for business taxes. The downside: you're handing your SSN to every client who asks for a W-9.

## Using an EIN
An EIN is a federal tax ID for your business. It's **free from the IRS**, and the IRS warns you "never have to pay a fee" ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)). Freelancers often get one to:
- Avoid sharing their SSN on W-9s
- Open a business bank account (many banks ask for one)
- Prepare for hiring or forming an LLC

Per the IRS, you generally *need* an EIN to hire employees or operate a partnership or corporation ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)).
