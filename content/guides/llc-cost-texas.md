---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Texas?"
date: 2026-10-14
---
The core state cost of forming a Texas LLC is the **$300 filing fee** for the Certificate of Formation (Form 205), as printed on the [official Texas Secretary of State form](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/forms/205_boc.pdf).

## What you pay the state
- **Certificate of Formation (Form 205): $300.** Source: [Texas SOS Form 205](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/forms/205_boc.pdf).
- Optional extras like name reservations or certified copies cost extra. Check the Secretary of State's current fee schedule before filing.

## What you might pay others
- **Registered agent:** free if you act as your own (you need a Texas street address). Paid services usually charge a yearly fee.
- **Formation service:** optional. - **EIN:** free from the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number).

## Ongoing costs
Texas LLCs deal with the Texas Comptroller for franchise tax reports. Check the Comptroller's site for your filing obligations, since requirements depend on revenue.

**Bottom line:** at least $300 if you file yourself. Always confirm on the official form, because fees can change.
