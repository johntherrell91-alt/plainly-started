---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Wisconsin?"
date: 2026-10-23
---
Forming a Wisconsin LLC costs **$130 online** (plus a $1 portal fee) or **$170 on paper**. After that, the annual report is **$25 a year online** or **$40 on paper**.

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $130 online through the One Stop Business Portal, plus a $1.00 portal fee, or $170 on paper.** Sources: [Wisconsin DFI fee table](https://dfi.wi.gov/Pages/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/Fees.aspx) and [Wisconsin One Stop Business Portal](https://onestop.wi.gov/OpenMyBusiness).
- **Expedited processing: optional, $100** for next-business-day handling. Source: [DFI fee table](https://dfi.wi.gov/Pages/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/Fees.aspx).
- **Annual report: $25 online, $40 on paper.** Since March 2024, paper filings carry a $15 surcharge. Sources: [DFI fee table](https://dfi.wi.gov/Pages/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/Fees.aspx) and [Form 5 instructions](https://dfi.wi.gov/Documents/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/Forms/CORP5i.pdf).
- **Change of registered agent or office: $10 online, $25 on paper.** Source: [DFI fee table](https://dfi.wi.gov/Pages/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/Fees.aspx).

## When the annual report is due
Your first report is due the year after you form, and then every year, by the **end of the calendar quarter that contains your formation anniversary**. For example, an LLC formed in May files by June 30 each year. Missing it can lead to administrative dissolution. Sources: [Wis. Stat. § 183.0212](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/scroll/up/613/statutes/statutes/183) and [Form 5 instructions](https://dfi.wi.gov/Documents/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/Forms/CORP5i.pdf).

## Registered agent
Your Articles must name a registered agent with a Wisconsin address, and Wisconsin also asks for the agent's email address. ## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$131** to form online, then **$25 a year** for the annual report starting the following year. Filing everything online saves about $55 over paper in the first two years. For a reminder of how annual reports work in general, see [LLC annual reports](../llc-annual-report/). Confirm current fees with the [Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions](https://dfi.wi.gov/Pages/BusinessServices/BusinessEntities/Fees.aspx), since they can change.
