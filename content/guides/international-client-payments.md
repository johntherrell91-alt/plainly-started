## Options to compare
- **Bank wire:** reliable, but fees on both ends and the exchange-rate markup can add up.
- **Multi-currency accounts** (e.g. <a href="#AFFILIATE-wise" rel="sponsored nofollow">Wise</a> <!-- PLACEHOLDER affiliate link: replace after approval -->): let you receive in foreign currencies, often with transparent conversion fees.
- **PayPal / card processors:** convenient for clients, but check the currency-conversion and cross-border fees.

## How to compare honestly
Look at the **total cost**: the fixed fee, plus the percentage fee, plus the gap between the rate you get and the mid-market rate. Run the same test amount through each provider's calculator.

## Tips
- Invoice in the client's currency if it helps them pay faster, and make sure you know who absorbs fees.
- State on the invoice who pays transfer fees.
