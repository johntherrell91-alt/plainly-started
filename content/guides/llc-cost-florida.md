---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Florida?"
date: 2026-10-14
---
According to the [Florida Division of Corporations fee page](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/forms/fees/llc-fees/):

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Filing fee (required) | $100.00 |
| Registered agent designation fee (required) | $25.00 |
| **Total for a new Florida LLC** | **$125.00** |
| Annual report | $138.75 |
| Annual report received after May 1 | $538.75 |

## The annual report trap
Florida's annual report jumps from $138.75 to **$538.75** if it's received after May 1 ([source](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/forms/fees/llc-fees/)). Put it on your calendar.
