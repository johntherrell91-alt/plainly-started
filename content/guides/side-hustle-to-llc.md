title: "Side hustle to LLC: when is it time to make it official?"
date: 2026-10-14

Signs it's time:
- **Steady income**: the side hustle earns regularly, not occasionally.
- **Client contracts with risk**: you could be sued over the work.
- **Clients ask for it**: some companies prefer to contract with a business entity.
- **You want separation**: separate bank account, branding, books.

Signs you can wait:
- You're still testing whether anyone will pay.
- Your state's ongoing LLC costs would eat most of your profit.

## Next steps
1. Price your state's filing and annual fees on the official Secretary of State site.
2. Read [LLC vs sole proprietorship](../llc-vs-sole-proprietor/).
3. Get your free EIN after forming. See [our EIN guide](../get-ein-free/).
