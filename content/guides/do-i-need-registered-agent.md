---
title: "Do I need a registered agent, and can I be my own?"
date: 2026-10-14
---
Every state requires an LLC to name a **registered agent**: a person or company with a physical address in the state who accepts legal papers and official mail during business hours.

## Can I be my own?
Usually yes, if you're an adult with a physical street address in the state (not a P.O. box) and you're reliably there during business hours.

## Reasons people pay for one
- **Privacy:** the agent's address goes on public records instead of your home address.
- **Availability:** you won't miss a lawsuit notice while traveling.
- **Multi-state:** you need an agent in each state where you register.

## Options
- Yourself: free.
- A friend or relative with an in-state address: free, but they must be dependable.
