---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in New Jersey?"
date: 2026-10-20
---
Forming a New Jersey LLC costs **$100** for the Certificate of Formation, then **$75 a year** for the annual report, per the [NJ Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services fee schedule](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/fees.shtml).

## What you pay the state
- **Certificate of Formation: $100.** You can file it online. Source: [NJ DORES fee schedule](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/fees.shtml) and [Getting Registered](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/gettingregistered.shtml).
- **Tax/employer registration (Form NJ-REG):** after the Certificate of Formation, New Jersey asks you to file the NJ-REG online. Once both filings are done you can get a Business Registration Certificate. Source: [Getting Registered](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/gettingregistered.shtml).
- **Annual report: $75.** Source: [NJ DORES fee schedule](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/fees.shtml) and [Business.NJ.gov](https://business.nj.gov/pages/filings-and-accounting).
- **Name reservation: $50,** optional. Source: [NJ DORES fee schedule](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/fees.shtml).
- **Change of registered agent or office: $25.** Source: [NJ DORES fee schedule](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/fees.shtml).

## When the annual report is due
Every year by the **last day of the month** in which you formed the LLC. It's your job to file even if the state never sends a reminder, and missing reports can lead to revocation. Source: [Business.NJ.gov](https://business.nj.gov/pages/filings-and-accounting) and [annual report page](https://account.business.nj.gov/filings/annual-report).

## Registered agent
Your Certificate of Formation names one registered agent and a **New Jersey street address** for the registered office (a PO Box only if the street address is listed too). Source: [NJ public records filing instructions (PDF)](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/pdf/NJ_Public_Records_Filing.pdf).

## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$100** to form, then **$75 a year** for the annual report. Confirm current fees with the [New Jersey Division of Revenue](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/fees.shtml) before filing, since they can change.
