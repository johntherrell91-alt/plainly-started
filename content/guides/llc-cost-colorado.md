---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Colorado?"
date: 2026-10-18
---
Forming a Colorado LLC costs **$50** to file Articles of Organization online, and **$25 a year** for the periodic report, per the [Colorado Secretary of State fee schedule](https://www.coloradosos.gov/pubs/info_center/fees/business.html). That makes Colorado one of the cheapest states to both start and keep an LLC.

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $50.** Colorado filings are online only. Source: [Colorado SOS fee schedule](https://www.coloradosos.gov/pubs/info_center/fees/business.html).
- **Periodic Report: $25 a year.** The fee rose from $10 to $25 on July 1, 2024. Source: [Colorado SOS news release](https://www.coloradosos.gov/pubs/newsRoom/pressReleases/2024/PR20240617Fees.html).
- **Late filing penalty: $50** on top of the report fee. Source: [Colorado SOS fee schedule](https://www.coloradosos.gov/pubs/info_center/fees/business.html).
- **Name reservation: $25,** optional, only if you want to hold a name before filing. Source: [Colorado SOS fee schedule](https://www.coloradosos.gov/pubs/info_center/fees/business.html).

## When the periodic report is due
Colorado doesn't use one statewide deadline. Each LLC has a "periodic report month," shown on its Summary page in the state's business database. You can file **two months before through two months after** that month without a penalty. Source: [Colorado SOS periodic report FAQ](https://www.coloradosos.gov/pubs/business/FAQs/reports.html).

If you don't file, the LLC's status becomes "Delinquent." Getting back to good standing means filing a Statement Curing Delinquency, which costs **$100**. Sources: [periodic report FAQ](https://www.coloradosos.gov/pubs/business/FAQs/reports.html) and [fee schedule](https://www.coloradosos.gov/pubs/info_center/fees/business.html). Signing up for the state's free email reminders is the easiest way to avoid that.

## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$50** to form. Then **$25 a year** for the periodic report. Confirm current fees with the [Colorado Secretary of State](https://www.coloradosos.gov/pubs/info_center/fees/business.html) before filing, since they can change.
