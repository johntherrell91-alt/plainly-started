---
title: "California's $800 LLC annual tax explained"
date: 2026-10-14
---
Every LLC "doing business or organized in California must pay an annual tax of $800", according to the [California Franchise Tax Board](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/limited-liability-company/index.html).

## Key facts (from FTB)
- It's due **even if you don't do business**, until you cancel the LLC.
- First payment: by the **15th day of the 4th month** after you file with the Secretary of State.
- Pay with Form FTB 3522, online via Web Pay, or by credit card.
- The first-year exemption only applied to tax years beginning 2021 through 2023, so it **no longer applies** to new LLCs.

## The separate LLC fee
If total California income is $250,000 or more, an additional fee applies, starting at $900 ([FTB table](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/limited-liability-company/index.html)).

## Don't forget
File the Statement of Information with the Secretary of State. FTB notes a **$250 penalty** if you don't.
