---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Georgia?"
date: 2026-10-15
---
Forming a Georgia LLC costs **$110** in state fees: a $100 filing fee plus a $10 service charge for the Articles of Organization, according to the [Georgia Secretary of State fee schedule](https://sos.ga.gov/sites/default/files/forms/Reference%20-%20Filing%20Fees_0.pdf).

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $110** ($100 filing fee + $10 service charge). Source: [Georgia SOS filing fees](https://sos.ga.gov/sites/default/files/forms/Reference%20-%20Filing%20Fees_0.pdf).
- **Annual registration: $60 per year** ($50 + $10 service charge), from the same fee schedule.
- Optional extras such as expedited service or certificates cost more. Check the fee schedule before you file.

## When the annual registration is due
Georgia LLCs file their **first annual registration between January 1 and April 1 of the year after formation**, then between January 1 and April 1 every year after that, per the [Georgia SOS Business Division FAQ](https://sos.ga.gov/page/business-division-faq). Georgia also lets you register for one, two, or three years at a time, paying the fee for each year ([Georgia rule 590-7-22](https://rules.sos.ga.gov/gac/590-7-22)).

So if you form your LLC in October, you won't owe the first $60 until the January 1 to April 1 window of next year.
