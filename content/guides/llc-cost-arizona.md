---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Arizona?"
date: 2026-10-18
---
Forming an Arizona LLC costs **$50** in state fees for Articles of Organization, or **$85** if you pay for expedited processing, per the [Arizona Corporation Commission fee schedule (PDF)](https://azcc.gov/docs/default-source/corps-files/fee-schedules/fee-schedule-llcs.pdf). Arizona also has **no annual report for LLCs**, which makes it one of the cheapest states to keep an LLC running.

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $50** regular, **$85** expedited. File with the Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC). Source: [ACC fee schedule (PDF)](https://azcc.gov/docs/default-source/corps-files/fee-schedules/fee-schedule-llcs.pdf).
- **Faster options cost a lot more.** The ACC lists add-ons of $100 for next-day, $200 for same-day, and $400 for two-hour service on top of the filing fee. Source: [ACC Articles of Organization instructions (PDF)](https://azcc.gov/docs/default-source/corps-files/instructions/l010i-instructions-articles-of-organization.pdf).
- **Annual report: none.** The ACC says plainly that LLCs aren't required to file annual reports; only corporations are. Source: [ACC Business Services FAQs](https://azcc.gov/faqs/BusinessServicesFAQs).

## The publication requirement (and how to skip the cost)
Arizona is one of the few states that requires you to publish notice of your new LLC. Within **60 days** after the ACC files your articles, a notice must run in a newspaper in the county of your statutory agent's street address for **three consecutive publications**. Source: [A.R.S. 29-3201](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/29/03201.htm).

The good news: **if your statutory agent's street address is in Maricopa County (Phoenix area) or Pima County (Tucson area), you don't pay for a newspaper.** The ACC posts the notice on its own website automatically. Don't publish until the ACC approves your articles. Source: [ACC instructions (PDF)](https://azcc.gov/docs/default-source/corps-files/instructions/l010i-instructions-articles-of-organization.pdf).

Outside those two counties, newspaper rates vary, so ask a local paper for a quote before you file.

## Statutory agent
Arizona calls it a "statutory agent." Every LLC must have one with a place of business or residence in Arizona. It can be an Arizona resident individual (including you) or a business authorized in Arizona, and the agent has to accept the appointment. Source: [A.R.S. 29-3115](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/29/03115.htm).

## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$50** to form, plus newspaper costs only if your statutory agent is outside Maricopa and Pima counties. After that, **$0 a year** in state report fees. Confirm current fees with the [Arizona Corporation Commission](https://azcc.gov/corporations) before filing, since they can change.
