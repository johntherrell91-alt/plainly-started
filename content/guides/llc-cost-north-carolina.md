---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in North Carolina?"
date: 2026-10-16
---
Forming a North Carolina LLC costs **$125** in state fees for Articles of Organization, as set in [N.C.G.S. § 57D-1-22](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_57D/GS_57D-1-22.html) and listed by the [North Carolina Secretary of State](https://www.sosnc.gov/manual/launching_a_business/preparing_the_document_and_attachments).

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $125.** File form L-01 (or online equivalent) with the NC Secretary of State. Source: [SOSNC preparing documents](https://www.sosnc.gov/manual/launching_a_business/preparing_the_document_and_attachments) and [G.S. 57D-1-22](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_57D/GS_57D-1-22.html).
- **Annual report: $200 by paper, or $203 online** (the online total includes a small electronic payment fee). Source: [SOSNC annual report FAQ](https://www.sosnc.gov/frequently_asked_questions/by_title/_BRD_FAQ_Annual_Reports). The statute lists the base annual report fee as $200 ([G.S. 57D-1-22](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_57D/GS_57D-1-22.html)).

## When the annual report is due
Per [G.S. 57D-2-24](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_57D/GS_57D-2-24.html) and the [SOSNC FAQ](https://www.sosnc.gov/frequently_asked_questions/by_title/_BRD_FAQ_Annual_Reports), the **first annual report is due by April 15 of the year after formation**, then every April 15 after that. So an LLC formed in October 2026 would owe its first report by April 15, 2027.

## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$125** to form. Then roughly **$200–$203 a year** for the annual report starting the April 15 after the year you formed. Confirm fees on the official SOSNC pages before filing, since they can change.
