---
title: "How to file an LLC annual report (and what if you miss it)"
date: 2026-10-14
---
Most states require LLCs to file a periodic report (often annual or biennial) confirming addresses, managers, and the registered agent. The name varies: "annual report", "Statement of Information", "franchise tax report".

## Steps
1. Find your state's Secretary of State (or equivalent) business portal.
2. Look up your LLC and note the due date and fee.
3. File online and keep the confirmation.

## If you miss it
States commonly charge late penalties, and eventually may administratively dissolve or suspend the LLC. For example, California's FTB notes a $250 penalty for not filing the Statement of Information. Reinstatement is usually possible but costs more than filing on time.

Tip: set a yearly calendar reminder, or use a compliance-reminder service.
