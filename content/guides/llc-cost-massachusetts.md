---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Massachusetts?"
date: 2026-10-20
---
Forming a Massachusetts LLC costs **$500** for the Certificate of Organization, then **$500 a year** for the annual report. Both amounts are set by state law in [M.G.L. c. 156C, §12](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXII/Chapter156c/Section12), which makes Massachusetts one of the more expensive states to keep an LLC in.

## What you pay the state
- **Certificate of Organization: $500.** Source: [Mass.gov: starting an LLC](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/starting-a-limited-liability-company-llc-in-massachusetts) and [Corporations Division LLC information](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/filing-by-subject/limited-liability/corporations-limited-liability-company.htm).
- **Annual report: $500.** Source: [Corporations Division filing fees](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/general-information/corporations-filing-fees.htm).
- **Name reservation: $30,** optional. Source: [Corporations Division filing fees](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/general-information/corporations-filing-fees.htm).
- **Change of resident agent or office:** no fee if filed electronically, $25 on paper or fax. Source: [Corporations Division filing fees](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/general-information/corporations-filing-fees.htm).

## When the annual report is due
On or before the **anniversary date** of the original Certificate of Organization filing, every year. Source: [Corporations Division LLC information](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/corporations/filing-by-subject/limited-liability/corporations-limited-liability-company.htm).

## Resident (registered) agent
Massachusetts calls it a **resident agent**. The Certificate of Organization must list an office address in Massachusetts plus the resident agent's name and address, with the agent's written consent in or attached to the certificate. Source: [M.G.L. c. 156C, §12](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXII/Chapter156c/Section12).
