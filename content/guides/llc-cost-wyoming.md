---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Wyoming?"
date: 2026-10-14
---
From the [Wyoming Secretary of State fee schedule (PDF)](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf):

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Articles of Organization | $100.00 |
| Certificate of Authority (out-of-state LLC registering in Wyoming) | $150.00 |
| Amendment / dissolution / other filing | $60.00 |
| Reinstatement for tax | $100.00 |
| Reinstatement for no registered agent | $350.00 |

## Annual report
The fee schedule states the annual report license tax is **$60 or two-tenths of one mill on the dollar ($.0002), whichever is greater**, based on assets as described on the schedule. Confirm your amount with the [official schedule](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/BusinessFees.pdf).

## Is Wyoming right for you?
Wyoming is popular for low fees and privacy. But if you live and work in another state, you'll likely have to register there too and pay that state's fees as well. Read [our home-state guide](../best-state-for-llc/) first.
