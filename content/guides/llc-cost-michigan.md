---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Michigan?"
date: 2026-10-17
---
Forming a Michigan LLC costs **$50** in state fees for Articles of Organization, as set in [MCL 450.5101](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-450-5101). That makes Michigan one of the cheapest states to start an LLC.

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $50.** File form CSCL/CD-700 online through the [MiBusiness Registry Portal](https://www.michigan.gov/lara/bureau-list/cscl/corps/frequently-asked-questions). Source: [MCL 450.5101](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-450-5101).
- **Annual statement: $25** through September 30, 2027. The statute drops it to **$15** for statements paid after that date. Source: [MCL 450.5101](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-450-5101).
- **Late penalty: $50** if the annual statement isn't filed or paid by February 15. Source: [LARA LLC filing information (PDF)](https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/lara/cscl/Folder1/BCS%5FCD%5F265.pdf).

## When the annual statement is due
Every Michigan LLC files an annual statement **by February 15** each year, starting the year after formation. There's a useful quirk: **if you form after September 30, you skip the very next February 15 statement.** So an LLC formed in October 2026 wouldn't owe one until February 15, 2028. Source: [LARA annual filings](https://www.michigan.gov/lara/bureau-list/cscl/corps/limited-liability-co/filling-requirements-continued/annual-filings).

If you miss filings for two years, the LLC falls out of good standing and its name can be taken by someone else, per [LARA](https://www.michigan.gov/lara/bureau-list/cscl/corps/limited-liability-co/filling-requirements-continued/annual-filings).

## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$50** to form. Then **$25 a year** for the annual statement (falling to $15 after September 2027). Confirm fees with [LARA](https://www.michigan.gov/lara/bureau-list/cscl/corps/frequently-asked-questions) before filing, since they can change.
