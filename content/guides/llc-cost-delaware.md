## The annual tax
All LLCs formed or registered in Delaware must pay an **annual tax of $400**, due **on or before June 1**. There's no annual report requirement for LLCs ([Delaware Division of Corporations](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/)).

Late payment brings a **$200 penalty plus 1.5% interest per month** on tax and penalty ([source](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/)). There's no proration: if the LLC is active at any point in the year, the tax applies.

## Do you also pay your home state?
If you live and work elsewhere, your home state will usually require you to register there as a foreign LLC too. See [Best state to form an LLC if you work from home](../best-state-for-llc/).

## Watch for scams
Delaware warns about deceptive mail and emails demanding fees. Trust only correspondence from the state or your registered agent ([source](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/)).
