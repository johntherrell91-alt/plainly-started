---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Ohio?"
date: 2026-10-15
---
Forming an Ohio LLC costs **$99**: the filing fee for Articles of Organization (Form 610), as listed on the [Ohio Secretary of State fee schedule](https://www.ohiosos.gov/businesses/filing-forms--fee-schedule/).

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization (Form 610): $99.** You can file online through Ohio Business Central or by mail. Source: [Ohio SOS fee schedule](https://www.ohiosos.gov/businesses/filing-forms--fee-schedule/).
- **Statutory agent update: $25**, only if you change your agent later.
- **Certificate of amendment: $50**, only if you change your articles later.

## You need a statutory agent
Ohio calls its registered agent a **statutory agent**. Your articles must name the agent and include the agent's signed acceptance of the appointment ([Ohio Revised Code 1706.16](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-1706.16) and [1706.09](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-1706.09)). You can serve as your own agent if you're an Ohio resident with a street address, or use a commercial service like <a href="#AFFILIATE-northwest" rel="sponsored nofollow">Northwest Registered Agent</a> <!-- PLACEHOLDER affiliate link: replace after approval --> for a yearly fee. More on that in [do I need a registered agent?](../do-i-need-registered-agent/)

## Ongoing costs
Ohio's Secretary of State fee schedule doesn't list a yearly annual report for domestic LLCs, which keeps Ohio among the cheaper states to maintain an LLC. Taxes are separate: check the [Ohio Department of Taxation](https://tax.ohio.gov/) for whether your business owes the Commercial Activity Tax or other state taxes based on your revenue.

## What you might pay others
- **Formation service:** optional. Services such as <a href="#AFFILIATE-zenbusiness" rel="sponsored nofollow">ZenBusiness</a> <!-- PLACEHOLDER affiliate link: replace after approval --> file for you and charge their package price *on top of* the $99 state fee.
- **EIN:** free from the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number).

**Bottom line:** $99 if you file yourself and act as your own statutory agent. Confirm the fee on the official schedule before filing, since fees can change.
