---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Washington?"
date: 2026-10-19
---
Forming a Washington LLC costs **$180** for a Certificate of Formation, then **$70 a year** for the annual report, per the [Washington Secretary of State fee schedule](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/frequently-asked-questions-faqs/fee-schedules-priority-services). Online filings also add an online processing fee; mail or in-person stays at the stated filing amounts.

## What you pay the state
- **Certificate of Formation: $180.** File online through the Corporations and Charities Filing System (CCFS) or by mail/in person. Online adds a processing fee on top of the $180. Expedited service is an extra **$100**. Sources: [WA SOS fee schedule](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/frequently-asked-questions-faqs/fee-schedules-priority-services) and [start a domestic LLC online](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/business-entities/online-filing-instructions/start-domestic-wa-limited-liability-company-llc-online).
- **Initial report:** due within **120 days** of the effective date. You can include it with the Certificate of Formation at **no extra fee**, or defer it and pay **$10** later. Source: [Certificate of Formation instructions (PDF)](https://www.sos.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/6.2025%20-%20Certificate%20of%20Formation%20-%20LLC%20%28Fillable%20Form%29.pdf) and the [online filing instructions](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/business-entities/online-filing-instructions/start-domestic-wa-limited-liability-company-llc-online).
- **Annual report: $70.** If the LLC is already delinquent, the fee is **$95** ($70 plus a $25 delinquency fee). Source: [WA SOS fee schedule](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/frequently-asked-questions-faqs/fee-schedules-priority-services) and [file an annual report online](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/business-entities/online-filing-instructions/file-annual-report-multiple-entity-types-online).
- **Name reservation: $30,** optional. Source: [WA SOS fee schedule](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/frequently-asked-questions-faqs/fee-schedules-priority-services).

## When the annual report is due
The annual report is due by the **last day of the month** in which the LLC was first formed. You can file up to **180 days** before that date. Source: [file an annual report online](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/business-entities/online-filing-instructions/file-annual-report-multiple-entity-types-online).

## Registered agent
Every Washington LLC must designate and keep a registered agent with a **physical street address in Washington** (no PO Boxes or PMBs) and an email address. You can serve as a noncommercial agent if you qualify and consent, or hire a commercial registered agent listed in CCFS. Source: [WA SOS registered agents](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/business-entities/maintain-business-compliance/registered-agents) and [RCW 23.95.415](https://apps.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=23.95.415).

## First-year total if you do it yourself
About **$180** to form (plus any online processing fee if you file online), if you include the initial report with formation. Then **$70 a year** for the annual report. Confirm current fees with the [Washington Secretary of State](https://www.sos.wa.gov/corporations-charities/frequently-asked-questions-faqs/fee-schedules-priority-services) before filing, since they can change.
