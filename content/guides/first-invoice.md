## What every invoice should include
1. Your business name and contact info
2. Client name and address
3. Unique invoice number (e.g. 2026-001)
4. Invoice date and due date
5. Itemized services: description, hours or quantity, rate
6. Total due, plus any tax if applicable
7. Payment methods and terms (e.g. "Net 15")

## Simple template
```
INVOICE #2026-001        Date: Oct 14, 2026   Due: Oct 29, 2026
From: [Your Business]     To: [Client]
Website redesign, 20 hrs × $50        $1,000.00
TOTAL DUE                              $1,000.00
Pay by: bank transfer / card link
```

- Agree on terms *before* you start work.
