---
title: "How to get an EIN for free (and when you don't need one)"
date: 2026-10-14
---
An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is your business's federal tax ID. **It is always free** from the IRS. Their own page warns: "You never have to pay a fee for an EIN." ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number))

## How to apply online
1. Form your LLC with your state first. The IRS says applying before forming "may be delayed".
2. Use the IRS online tool. It must be done in one session and times out after 15 minutes of inactivity.
3. Print or save the confirmation letter.

You need the responsible party's SSN or ITIN, and you can get one EIN per responsible party per day. ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number))

## When you need one
Per the IRS, generally when you hire employees, operate a partnership or corporation, pay certain sales/excise taxes, or change structure. A single-member LLC with no employees may be able to use the owner's SSN for federal income tax, though many banks ask for an EIN to open a business account.
