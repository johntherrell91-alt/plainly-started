---
title: "How much does it cost to form an LLC in Missouri?"
date: 2026-10-23
---
Forming a Missouri LLC costs **$50 if you file online** or **$105 on paper**. Missouri has **no annual report for LLCs**, so there's no yearly state filing fee after that. That makes Missouri one of the cheapest states to form and keep an LLC.

## What you pay the state
- **Articles of Organization: $50 online, $105 on paper.** Online filings are paid by credit card. Sources: [Missouri Secretary of State fee schedule](https://www.sos.mo.gov/CMSImages/Business/fees.pdf?v=2025) and [Missouri business FAQs](https://www.sos.mo.gov/business/faqs).
- **Amendments: $25** if you change your Articles later. Source: [fee schedule](https://www.sos.mo.gov/CMSImages/Business/fees.pdf?v=2025).
- **Fictitious name (DBA): $7** if you want to do business under a name other than your LLC's legal name. Source: [fee schedule](https://www.sos.mo.gov/CMSImages/Business/fees.pdf?v=2025).

## Is there an annual report?
No. The Secretary of State says plainly that Missouri LLCs don't have to file an annual report. Keep your registered agent information current, though, because changes are filed separately. Source: [Missouri business FAQs](https://www.sos.mo.gov/business/faqs).

## Free filing for active-duty military
Active-duty service members in Missouri, including the Missouri National Guard, can form a business without paying the Secretary of State filing fee. Source: [fee schedule](https://www.sos.mo.gov/CMSImages/Business/fees.pdf?v=2025).

About **$50** if you file online, with no yearly state report fee after that. You'll still owe normal federal and Missouri income taxes on your profit.
