Plainly Started publishes plain-English guides for people turning a skill or side hustle into a real business. We link to official government sources whenever we mention fees or rules, and we update guides when those sources change.

We are an independent publication. We are not lawyers, accountants, or tax advisors, and nothing here is legal or tax advice. For decisions specific to your situation, consult a licensed professional or your state's official business office.
